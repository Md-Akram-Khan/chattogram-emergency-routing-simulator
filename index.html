Real-World Dynamic Cost & Heuristic Model for Chattogram Metro

Chattogram's extended network of 45 high-density nodes incorporates key hospitals (CMCH, Evercare, Imperial, Max, General Hospital, BITID), port gates (Port Gate 1, Custom House, Saltgola, CEPZ), flyovers, coastal embankments in Halishahar, and northern highway bypasses.

1. Dynamic Edge Cost Function C(u, v)

The dynamic traversal cost for an emergency vehicle along road segment \((u, v)\) is formulated as:

C(u, v) = Distance(u, v) × w_road × PortLorryTraffic × TidalFloodFactor × SafetyFactor + JunctionDelay

2. Admissible Heuristic Function H(n)

For informed search algorithms (**A* Search** and **Greedy BFS**), the heuristic estimate \(H(n)\) uses straight-line Haversine distance scaled to maintain admissibility:

H(n, Goal) = HaversineDistance(n.lat, n.lng, Goal.lat, Goal.lng) × 0.8
